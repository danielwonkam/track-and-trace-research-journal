    Daily research journal — 2026-09-15¶

    Brief overview¶

    • What is being done? The reviewed workpiece recordings were evaluated, and the FTS, workpiece replay, dashboard and NFC input were brought together in a separate live-test branch.
    • What is the goal? Build one understandable demonstration that follows the FTS and its workpieces while keeping experimental results separate from live demonstration claims.
    • What are the consequences? Live FTS tracking and the MQTT connection work. The new recordings also showed that workpiece model v0.2 does not transfer reliably to the changed camera conditions, so it is not ready for deployment.
    • What are the next steps? Restore the exact detector and tracker pair from the successful two-piece feasibility test, connect it to live video, and test NFC-to-workpiece association in one synchronized physical run.

    Objective¶

    Verify completion and integrity of the targeted prospective workpiece annotation quality review without interpreting it as independent review or scoring release.

    Work completed¶

    • Read the live QA project and summary after the reviewer reported completion.
    • Audited the append-only v0.2 QA history, duplicate frame confirmations, unresolved-frame count and first-pass binding.
    • Confirmed that no annotation was corrected during the 60-frame review.

    Method and rationale¶

    The service reloaded and validated every QA history entry against the v0.2 snapshot, image hash, reviewer identity, baseline decision hash, previous-decision chain and explicit confirmation. Counts were computed from the complete log rather than inferred from the browser progress indicator. Duplicate entries were retained because the review store is append-only; deleting them would damage provenance.

    Results and evidence¶

    • Review version: workpiece-prospective-quality-review-v0.2.
    • Queue status: 60/60 unique frames reviewed, zero remaining and zero unresolved.
    • History: 63 append-only records for 60 frames; all 63 actions are confirmed and zero are corrected.
    • The three extra records are repeat confirmations of prospective_two_on_fts_replacement_w01_f001401. Its four records share one baseline hash and form a service-validated previous-decision chain.
    • QA log SHA-256: 04E7F802EF3606941185F38889F5E6C63FBA19C912A6DB466D0AC0738A01B1AD.
    • Bound first-pass SHA-256: 3A02464C966EFA743F881872FD510CB079E4F783D4EF7FEFA668FF00BB36563E.
    • The live API reports completion while preserving independent_review = false and scoring_authorized = false.

    Problems, mistakes, and diagnosis¶

    The last frame was saved four times, producing three redundant confirmations. They are not corrections: all use the same baseline, and the latest-per-frame projection still contains exactly 60 reviewed frames. The review service accepted the complete chain, so no evidence repair or deletion was performed.

    Corrections or decisions¶

    No human annotation or QA record was modified. The completed state is recorded as evidence; the earlier decision to keep targeted QA separate from scoring remains active (D-023).

    Limitations¶

    Agreement with the original labels is same-reviewer confirmation, not an independent accuracy measurement. The visual QA covered only the 60-frame replacement sequence, not the other 300 prospective frames. Completion does not authorize opening predictions, calculating metrics or tuning a model.

    Next step¶

    Diagnose the prospective domain gap using development evidence only. Do not tune against the 300 evaluation-role frames. If the detector or tracker is changed, acquire a new unseen source before making another prospective performance claim.

    Provisional prospective evaluation¶

    After QA completion, Daniel Wonkam explicitly authorized a single-reviewer protocol deviation because no independent reviewer was available. Before any prediction row was read, the release froze both execution seals, both manifests, the first-pass and QA histories, evaluator implementations, IoU 0.50, detector confidence 0.20, the 300/60 role split and the existing research gates.

    The one-time evaluator read only the two previously sealed prediction streams; it did not run YOLO or the tracker. All 300 evaluation-role frames and all 60 development-reserve frames were evaluated, with zero ambiguous exclusions. Predictions were not regenerated.

    Evaluation-role observations:

    • Detector: precision 0.3285, recall 0.0687, F1 0.1137, macro F1 0.1193 and AP50 0.0412.
    • Detector recall by colour: red 0.0417, blue 0.1161 and white 0.0580.
    • Tracker: precision 0.2777, recall/temporal coverage 0.0740 and F1 0.1169.
    • Tracker coverage by colour: red 0.0417, blue 0.1223 and white 0.0681.
    • Tracker identity evidence: five ID switches, zero simultaneous identity swaps and 15 track fragments.
    • Frozen checks: detector macro F1 failed, every-colour detector recall failed, every-colour tracker coverage failed and the zero-swap check passed.

    Development-reserve observations, reported separately:

    • Detector macro F1 0.2230; recall red 0.0000, blue 0.2375, white 0.2000.
    • Tracker temporal coverage 0.1429; coverage red 0.0000, blue 0.2500, white 0.2000.

    These results indicate a large prospective transfer failure under the frozen protocol. They remain provisional because annotation quality was not reviewed independently and the targeted same-reviewer QA covered only the replacement sequence. The final prospective gate is blocked; this is not production certification and station-event scoring remains unauthorized.

    Research portal follow-up — readability and language access¶

    • Problem: dark mode applied a dark background while body and table text retained near-black foregrounds. The journal also lacked French and German body content, and the previous archive had not been checked offline.
    • Method and rationale: use Material's complete light and slate schemes; add static English, French and German pages rather than send private research to a translation service. The homepage and supervisor briefing are fully localized. Dated entries and reference pages have clearly labelled reading summaries, each linking to its complete English original.
    • Evidence: a strict multilingual build and regression/link tests passed. Browser checks covered 18 page/theme combinations, six narrow-screen views, language/page/theme preservation after reload, keyboard focus, reduced motion, and print colours. Dark table text measured about 14.6:1 contrast on cell backgrounds and 12.9:1 on headings. An extracted archive was checked in Edge with networking disabled: search in all three languages and English-original navigation worked, with no network requests or browser errors.
    • Limitations: French and German evidence pages are summaries, not complete translations or independent scientific review. The site is a dated snapshot, not live telemetry. Browser checks are not a full accessibility certification. The embedded VS Code browser restricted file-based search, so offline acceptance was checked separately in standard Edge. Old staging-only pages remain on disk but are excluded from the published evidence allowlist.
    • Consequence and next step: approved readers can use the extracted website without installation. Keep both reading summaries current when a new dated entry is added; the build must reject missing editions. No model, annotation, evaluation, or research-gate result changed through this presentation work.
