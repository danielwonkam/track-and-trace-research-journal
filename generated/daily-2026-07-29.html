    Daily research journal — 2026-07-29¶

    Objective¶

    Reduce manual boxing effort for Milestone 2 without allowing unverified AI output to become FTS ground truth.

    Work completed¶

    • Added five medium-confidence visual AI box proposals across representative pilot frames.
    • Added orange proposal overlays and explicit Accept, Adjust, Reject, and Next AI proposal controls to the browser annotation workspace.
    • Kept AI proposals separate from append-only human decisions and YOLO labels.
    • Added provenance fields that record whether a saved human decision accepted, adjusted, or rejected a proposal and which method supplied it.
    • Added a regression test that confirms a proposal is not ground truth.
    • Completed human review of all five proposals. Each was adjusted before save; none was accepted unchanged. At that point, 12/48 pilot frames had saved human decisions in total.
    • Compared the original and corrected proposal boxes: IoUs were 0.31, 0.47, 0.46, 0.00, and 0.44 (mean 0.34). This supports retaining the corrections as labels while rejecting the current visual proposal method for automatic use.
    • Completed the first annotation pass: 48/48 frames, 48 labels, and 48 latest decisions. The integrity audit found zero missing or inconsistent artifacts.
    • Observed 48 positives (46 clear, 2 partial) and zero negatives. This is a sampling limitation, not evidence that negatives do not occur.
    • Implemented and activated an append-only second-review queue for the 8 validation and 16 test frames. It supports separate approval, corrected geometry, and uncertainty flags while preserving first-pass provenance.
    • Completed the queue: 24/24 labels approved (8 validation, 16 test), with zero corrections or uncertainty flags. An audit found zero missing reviews, labels, or manifest verification states. Daniel Wonkam performed both passes, so this is documented as same-reviewer checking rather than independent verification.
    • Added a deterministic targeted-negative sampler and extracted the separate fts-negative-candidates-v0.1 project: initially 12 pending frames, two from each of the six recordings, preserving the train/validation/test source split.
    • Collapsed append-only baseline reviews to their latest decision before selection, preferred reviewed detector-error timestamps, and used explicit recording-endpoint fallbacks only when a source lacked two distinct errors.
    • Confirmed that extraction created no labels or annotation decisions. The initial dashboard state was 0/12 annotated and required human whole-frame inspection.
    • Added three sampler regression tests.
    • Audited the candidate artifact: 12 distinct readable 1920×1080 images, 12 recorded checksums, 6 train/2 validation/4 test rows, 11 reviewed detector- error timestamps, one endpoint fallback, and zero labels or decisions.
    • Changed the dashboard to say Candidate — not a verified negative, expose selection evidence separately from editable annotator notes, and disable second review until every candidate has a first-pass decision.
    • Generalized review correction so a reviewer can replace either incorrect geometry or an incorrect class/visibility answer while retaining provenance.
    • Completed the candidate project during this work session: 12/12 first-pass decisions and 6/6 held-out reviews. All 12 were fts_visible and clear; all 6 held-out decisions were approved. An integrity audit found 12 non-empty valid labels and zero structural issues.
    • Rejected the targeted-negative sampling hypothesis for full-frame absence: the detector-error timestamps improved station-confuser background coverage, but produced 12 additional positives and zero verified negatives.
    • Inspected the supplied CHRG source recording: 66.133 s, 1920×1080 at 30 FPS. The FTS is parked at CHRG before later manual relocation into the route.
    • Added a deterministic CHRG supplement extractor and prepared six pending frames from 4.300–25.700 s. The entire source is train-only to prevent the manual-placement artifact from contaminating validation or test evaluation.
    • Audited the CHRG artifact: six unique readable images, six matching checksums, six immutable provenance rows, no labels, no decisions, and zero issues.
    • Completed and audited CHRG annotation: 6/6 clear FTS positives, six valid non-empty labels, five station_overlap tags, and zero integrity issues. The verified pool now contains 66 positives and 0 full-frame negatives.

    Method and rationale¶

    A five-frame calibration pilot was chosen instead of proposing boxes for all 48 frames. The frames cover different recordings, splits, occlusion conditions, image-edge placement, and blurred poses. A separate proposal file and orange overlay make the machine suggestion visible while preserving explicit human authority. Automatic label creation was rejected because it would contaminate the evaluation set and hide proposal errors.

    Results and evidence¶

    • Five proposals are stored in data/annotations/fts-pilot-v0.1/ai_proposals.jsonl.
    • Dashboard project state reported 0/48 completed before human review; proposal display does not change that count.
    • Browser inspection confirmed the first proposal rendered in orange and Accept converted it to cyan locally without changing completion progress.
    • Final validation after the immutable-provenance changes passed Ruff, browser JavaScript syntax checking, all 51 tests, and a strict MkDocs build.

    Problems, mistakes, and diagnosis¶

    Ordinary Next navigation stepped through all 48 frames, making the five proposals hard to review consecutively. A direct Next AI proposal action was added. The box coordinates were estimated from visual inspection and remain uncertain until the human reviewer checks each image; two diagonal-pose images appeared similar and require independent review.

    The candidate project initially exposed a validation/test second-review button while all 12 first-pass decisions were still pending. The API rejected an individual unannotated frame, but the browser affordance was misleading. The project-level gate now requires the complete first pass. Manifest selection notes were also hidden and would have been overwritten by an annotator note; they are now preserved as source_notes and displayed as read-only evidence.

    Corrections or decisions¶

    AI proposals remain read-only evidence until explicit human Save. Orange means unverified proposal and cyan means human-selected geometry. Proposal provenance is retained in the decision record, and direct proposal navigation avoids mistaking pending non-proposal frames for completed pilot review.

    Limitations¶

    The five corrected proposals do not establish annotation accuracy, detector precision, recall, frame-level performance, or the usefulness of AI assistance. No proposal was accepted unchanged, so the initial proposal method should not be scaled to the remaining frames without improvement.

    The completed annotation pool now contains 60 positives and no full-frame negative. Fixed stations are still present as unlabeled background inside the positive images, but this does not satisfy the explicit empty-label coverage requirement. Continuing to mine the same route recordings is not justified by the failed candidate result; an empty-shopfloor source clip is required.

    Next step¶

    Capture a short same-camera empty-shopfloor recording with the FTS physically outside the field of view and manually verify its station-dominated and open-floor frames before freezing the combined dataset.
