    Planification¶

    Created: 4 September 2026
    Immediate planning period: 4-11 September 2026
    Status: Provisional. The plan will be reviewed next week and may change after the first results from the new recordings.

    What is the goal now?¶

    The main technical work is close to completion. The FTS pipeline is available, and the new workpiece detector has finished training. The remaining goal is to check whether the system also works on genuinely new situations, especially when the FTS carries several workpieces. In parallel, I need to move from prototype development to thesis writing.

    What still needs to be done?¶

    1. Record new videos that were not used for training. They should include a simple one-piece route and realistic multi-piece configurations.
    2. Select and label a representative set of frames. Consecutive duplicate frames will be avoided so that the evaluation remains useful and manageable.
    3. Compare the previous and new workpiece models on exactly the same unseen frames.
    4. Evaluate whether several pieces can be detected, tracked, and assigned to the correct station history without identity switches.
    5. Decide from this evidence whether the technical work can be closed or whether one small, targeted correction is necessary.
    6. Begin the thesis structure and write the method and completed results while the final evaluation is being prepared.

    What is planned for today and next week?¶

    Date Planned work Estimated time
    Friday, 4 September Fix the remaining scope, define the new recording scenarios, and prepare the thesis outline 0.5-1 day
    Monday, 7 September Record and verify the unseen one-piece and multi-piece videos 0.5-1 day
    Tuesday, 8 September Select and label representative detector-evaluation frames 1 day
    Wednesday, 9 September Complete temporal identity labels where needed and compare both detector versions 1 day
    Thursday, 10 September Evaluate multi-piece tracking and station traceability; document failure cases 1 day
    Friday, 11 September Make the technical stop-or-correct decision and send a short results update 0.5-1 day

    How much time do I expect to need?¶

    If the unseen evaluation confirms the current result, I expect about five working days to complete the remaining core technical evaluation. If it reveals an important detector, tracking, or identity problem, I will reserve three to five additional working days for one targeted correction and a repeat evaluation.

    The thesis text has not yet been drafted as chapters. After the technical scope is frozen, I estimate three to four weeks for the first complete draft and one additional week for feedback, corrections, figures, and presentation preparation. The current overall estimate is therefore four to six weeks, depending mainly on the unseen multi-piece result and supervisor feedback.

    What do I need?¶

    • Access to the shopfloor and camera for the new recordings.
    • A realistic scenario with several workpieces on the FTS.
    • Agreement that new requirements discovered after the evaluation are optional unless they are necessary for the thesis question.
    • Early feedback on the thesis outline so that writing can continue without waiting for every optional prototype improvement.

    When will this plan be reviewed?¶

    I will review this plan after the evaluation work on 11 September 2026. Any change in scope or duration will be explained briefly rather than silently moving the dates.
