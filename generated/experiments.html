    Required fields for new experiments¶

    Every formal experiment must record:

    • a falsifiable question or comparison;
    • exact input session identifiers and dataset version;
    • configuration, model/weights identifier, seed where relevant, and code revision;
    • machine, interpreter, library versions, and runtime conditions;
    • quantitative results and representative successes and failures;
    • invalid or interrupted measurements;
    • a conclusion limited to what the annotation protocol supports; and
    • paths to machine-readable and human-readable outputs.
